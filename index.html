ABOUT CRADLE SAINTS

Welcome to Cradle Saints

At Cradle Saints, we provide our children with quality education in a welcoming and enabling environment where your children are given excellent care physically, emotionally and socially, using appropriate resources.

🎯 Our Mission

Adding value by preparing children for a future of success and distinction.

🌟 Our Vision

To be the choice of parents when it comes to giving our children the best start in life.

❤️ Our Values

We earnestly promote these values:

  1. Having the fear and love of God.
  2. Teaching the truth always.
  3. Respectfulness and Humility.

📚 Our Curriculum

The school's core curriculum focuses on numeracy, reading and character building.

We also have extracurricular activities such as sports, excursions, field trips, clubs and societies.
